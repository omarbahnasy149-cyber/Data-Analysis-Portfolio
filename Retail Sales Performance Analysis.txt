Project Name: Retail Sales Performance Analysis using Python
Objective: Analyze a retail sales dataset to identify top-performing product categories and regions to support data-driven inventory planning.

Key Insights:

1. Top Category: Electronics recorded the highest revenue share compared to Clothing and Home categories.

2. Regional Performance: The West region had the strongest sales performance.

3. Recommendation: Increase Electronics inventory levels in West-region warehouses to prevent stockouts and                          support demand growth. 

Tools Used: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib
