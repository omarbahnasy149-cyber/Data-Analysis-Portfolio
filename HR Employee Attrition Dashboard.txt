Project Name: HR Employee Attrition Dashboard
Objective: Develop a dashboard to identify departments with high turnover rates using Excel.

Key Insights:

1. High Risk: The Marketing department shows the highest attrition rate (40%), indicating workload or management issues.

2. Stability: HR has the lowest turnover rate.

3. Recommendation: Conduct exit interviews for Marketing staff to identify core dissatisfaction factors.

Tools Used: Excel, Data Visualization
